About DentPro

Trusted dental care since 2011

Get to know our story, the people behind your treatment, and everything you need to plan your visit.

Our Story

DentPro was founded in 2011 by Dr. Marat Yessenov with a simple goal: make quality dental care approachable for the whole family. What began as a two-chair practice has grown into a full-service clinic with eight specialist dentists, digital diagnostic equipment, and a reputation for calm, patient-first care.

Today we treat thousands of patients a year, from first baby teeth to full smile restorations, always guided by the same principle: explain clearly, treat gently, and never rush a decision that affects someone's health.

Our mission and values

Our Mission

Our Vision

Core Values

  • Transparency — clear pricing and honest treatment plans.
  • Comfort — a calm environment for anxious patients.
