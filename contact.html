Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a referral to book an appointment?

No referral is needed. You can book directly through this page or by calling our front desk.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Please bring a form of ID and any previous dental records or X-rays, if available.

Can I reschedule or cancel my appointment?

Yes, please contact us at least 24 hours in advance so we can offer the slot to another patient.

Do you treat dental emergencies?

Yes, we hold same-day slots for urgent issues such as severe pain, swelling or broken teeth.
